# Go Vita — Board Demo Runsheet (~20 minutes)

**Audience:** Board of Directors + Member Representatives
**Format:** You play a member and only ever message **Alice**. Behind the scenes Alice routes
to **Bob** (accounts) or **Cindy** (marketing) and brings you one consolidated reply.
**Live site:** https://bmcevoyau.github.io/pipes-demo-govita/

> All members, orders, pricing and assets are fictional demo data.

**Before you start:** make sure Alice, Bob and Cindy are all online, and have the website open
on screen. Type each prompt to Alice exactly as written (store identity is included so Alice can
look up the right account). Pause after each to let the audience see the routing happen.

---

## 0 · Open (2 min) — the idea
Show the **home page**. One line: *"110+ independently owned stores, one front door. A member
asks once; the right specialist answers every time — and the member never has to know who did what."*

---

## 1 · "Who do I ask?" — Alice answers directly (2½ min)
**Exercises:** Alice only (no escalation).

> **Prompt:** *"Hi, I've just taken over the Fremantle store and I'm not sure who handles what. Can you point me in the right direction, and tell me where I find your marketing materials?"*

**Expected:** Alice welcomes them, explains the routing (accounts → Bob, marketing → Cindy,
general → Alice) and links the marketing page.
**Why it lands** — *Board:* not everything needs a human or a hand-off; the front door self-serves.
*Members:* friendly, instant orientation.

---

## 2 · Pricing + place an order — Alice → Bob (3½ min)
**Exercises:** Alice clarifies → Bob (tier-aware pricing + raises order) → Alice relays.

> **Prompt:** *"Hi, it's Priya from Go Vita Bondi Junction. I'd like to reorder Magnesium Glycinate and Wild Fish Oil — can you give me my pricing and place an order for 10 cartons of each?"*

**Expected (Bondi = GV-1007, Gold tier −10%):**
- Magnesium Glycinate $18.50 ea · Fish Oil $21.00 ea (6 units/carton)
- 60 + 60 units → subtotal $2,370.00, Gold 10% −$237.00, **net $2,133.00**, free freight (>$300),
  GST $213.30, **total ≈ $2,346.30 inc GST**; order reference + ETA to Sydney Metro.
**Why it lands** — *Board:* accurate, tier-aware pricing and order capture, automatically.
*Members:* reorder in seconds, with your member discount already applied.

---

## 3 · "Where's my order?" — Alice → Bob (2½ min)
**Exercises:** Bob looks up a real order + tracking.

> **Prompt:** *"It's Megan at Go Vita Toowoomba — can you check the status of my last order and when it'll arrive?"*

**Expected:** Order **SO-24810**, **Dispatched** via TNT (tracking **TNT90552117**), $465.70,
lines incl. plant protein, BCAA, meal-replacement shakes; ETA relayed.
**Why it lands** — *Board:* real-time operational visibility. *Members:* no phone tag for a status check.

---

## 4 · When something goes wrong — Alice → Bob (3 min)  ⭐ credibility moment
**Exercises:** Bob handles an exception gracefully.

> **Prompt:** *"Hi, it's Oliver from Go Vita Ballarat — one of my orders hasn't moved, can you find out what's going on?"*

**Expected:** Order **SO-24820** is on **back-order** (BCAA Electrolytes Berry, qty 20). Bob
explains clearly, and offers options — ship the in-stock lines now and the rest when it lands,
give an ETA, or suggest a substitute. Alice relays with reassurance.
**Why it lands** — *Board:* the system is honest and helpful when things aren't perfect — that's
where trust is won. *Members:* proactive, no chasing.

---

## 5 · Seasonal marketing material — Alice → Cindy (4 min)
**Exercises:** Alice → Cindy (brand-consistent, ready-to-use assets + download links).

> **Prompt:** *"It's Megan again at Toowoomba — I want to freshen up the store for the new season. What campaign material do you have for in-store and social, and do you have the latest Go Mag?"*

**Expected:** Cindy returns current campaigns (**Spring Reset / Go Mag feature**, **Winter Wellness**)
with assets per channel — window posters, shelf-talkers, social tiles, web banner, logos, and the
current **Go Mag** cover — each with a live download link; offers to add the store name / tweak wording.
**Why it lands** — *Board:* consistent national brand across 110 independent stores, centrally
controlled. *Members:* professional-looking store and socials in minutes, free.

---

## 6 · Close (2 min) — the strategic picture
Quick tour of the three role pages + data on the live site, then recap:
- **One front door** for every member, 24/7, scaling to all 110+ stores
- **Accurate, tier-aware** accounts answers and order capture
- **Brand-consistent** marketing, centrally produced, instantly available
- **Honest exception handling** — the hard cases are handled, not hidden

---

### Overflow / backup prompts (if you have time or want variety)
- *"It's Tom at Fremantle — what multivitamin options do you have and what's my price?"* (Bob; shows
  the system being upfront when there's no exact SKU and recommending alternatives)
- *"It's Priya at Bondi Junction — can you send me the immune-support shelf talkers and a social tile?"* (Cindy)
- *"Hi, it's Hannah at Go Vita Noosa — what's my account's recent order history?"* (Bob)

### Presenter notes
- You only message Alice; Bob and Cindy stay behind the scenes.
- Expect a brief clarifying question from Alice on some prompts — that's the "understands before acting" step; it demos well.
- All figures above are computed from the live demo data and should match what Bob/Cindy return.
