# Go Vita — Board Demo Runsheet (~20 minutes)

**Audience:** Board of Directors + Member Representatives
**Format:** You play **one customer — Tom Harper, the new owner of Go Vita Fremantle (member GV-1012, Silver tier)** — and you only ever message **Alice**. Each request builds on the last, so the board sees one continuous member journey. Behind the scenes Alice routes to **Bob** (accounts) or **Cindy** (marketing) and brings back one consolidated reply.
**Live site:** https://bmcevoyau.github.io/pipes-demo-govita/

> All members, orders, pricing and assets are fictional demo data.

**Before you start:** make sure Alice, Bob and Cindy are online, and have the website open on
screen. Type each prompt to Alice (store identity is already known from Scene 1). Pause after each
to let the audience see the routing happen.

**The journey:** new owner gets oriented → asks about products & price → places first order →
is offered marketing to promote the new range → tracks the order → hits a back-order and sees it
handled → close.

---

## 0 · Open (2 min)
Show the **home page**. *"110+ independently owned stores, one front door. A member asks once; the
right specialist answers every time — and the member never has to know who did what. Today you're
Tom, who's just taken over our Fremantle store."*

---

## 1 · "Who do I ask?" — Alice answers directly (2 min)
> **Prompt:** *"Hi, I've just taken over the Fremantle store and I'm not sure who handles what. Can you point me in the right direction, and where do I find your marketing materials?"*

**Expected:** Alice welcomes Tom, explains routing (accounts → Bob, marketing → Cindy, general → Alice),
links the marketing page.
**Why it lands** — *Board:* the front door self-serves. *Members:* instant, friendly orientation.

---

## 2 · Products + price → place the order — Alice → Bob (4 min)
> **Prompt:** *"What multivitamin options do you have, and what's my price?"*

**Expected (Bob, upfront):** *"We don't stock a single multivitamin SKU — rather than sell you
something that isn't quite right, here's the immune foundation we'd recommend at your Silver price:"*
- Vitamin D3 1000IU (GV-VIT-0102) — **$12.13 ea** (RRP $24.95)
- Vitamin C 1000mg (GV-VIT-0103) — **$9.17 ea** (RRP $18.95)
- Zinc Picolinate 25mg (GV-VIT-0104) — **$7.90 ea** (RRP $16.50)

> **Then place the order:** *"Great — I'll take 15 cartons of the D3, 10 of the Vitamin C and 8 of the Zinc."*

**Expected:** Bob raises **SO-24825** — 15+10+8 cartons (90+80+64 units): subtotal $2,478.60,
Silver −$148.72, **net $2,329.88**, free freight, GST $232.99, **total $2,562.87 inc GST**; dispatch
+ ETA to follow. (The order appears on the accounts page.)

**➡ Alice's segue (she offers this proactively):** *"Since your new range is on its way, would you
like our latest marketing material so you can start promoting it in-store and online, ready for when
it arrives?"* → leads straight into Scene 3.

**Why it lands** — *Board:* the system is honest (won't force a non-existent SKU), recommends well,
prices by tier, and captures the order. *Members:* trustworthy advice + instant, discount-applied pricing.

---

## 3 · Marketing to promote the new range — Alice → Cindy (4 min)
> **Prompt:** *"Yes please — what have you got?"*

**Expected:** Cindy returns a pack tied to Tom's immune range, with live download links — leading with
**store-personalised "ARRIVING SOON · Go Vita Fremantle"** assets so he can promote before stock lands:
- ★ Personalised immune window poster + social tile reading **"Go Vita Fremantle"**
- Immune shelf-talker, poster, social tile, web banner (D3 + C + Zinc theme)
- Seasonal **Spring Reset** set + current **Go Mag** cover; logos (primary/reverse/Rewards)
- Cindy offers to tweak wording / swap "IN STORE NOW" ↔ "ARRIVING SOON" / add specific SKUs.

(Asset URLs live under `/assets/marketing/`; full catalogue at `/marketing.html`.)

**Why it lands** — *Board:* consistent national brand across 110 independent stores, centrally produced,
personalised in seconds. *Members:* a professional store + socials in minutes, free — promoting the exact
range they just bought, before it even arrives.

---

## 4 · "Where's my order?" — Alice → Bob (2½ min)
> **Prompt:** *"Thanks! Can you tell me when my order will arrive?"*

**Expected:** **SO-24825** is now **Dispatched** via StarTrack, tracking **ST4472651AU**, ETA
Fremantle / Perth Metro **Mon 19 – Wed 21 Oct 2026** (5–7 business days).
**Why it lands** — *Board:* real-time order visibility. *Members:* no phone tag for a status check.

---

## 5 · When something goes wrong — Alice → Bob (3 min)  ⭐ credibility moment
> **Prompt:** *"One more thing — I'd like to stock your BCAA electrolytes too. Can you add 20 cartons?"*

**Expected:** Bob raises **SO-24826** and is upfront: the **BCAA Electrolytes Berry (×20) is on
back-order**, but the in-stock lines can go now. Options:
- **(a) recommended:** ship the in-stock **8× ProFuel Plant Protein now** + BCAA to follow, **split
  freight waived** (parts $268.80 + $347.42; same $599.72 total)
- (b) hold & ship complete — BCAA restock ETA ~Mon 26 Oct
- (c) substitute BCAA with in-stock ProFuel Creatine 300g ($14.20 ea Silver)

Alice relays option (a) with reassurance.
**Why it lands** — *Board:* the hard case is handled honestly, not hidden — that's where trust is won.
*Members:* proactive, no chasing, no worse off on freight.

---

## 6 · Close (2 min)
Quick tour of the three role pages + data on the live site (Tom's SO-24825 is right there), then recap:
- **One front door**, 24/7, scaling to all 110+ stores
- **Honest, tier-aware** advice, pricing and order capture
- **Brand-consistent, personalised** marketing, instantly available
- **Graceful exception handling** — the hard cases are handled, not hidden

---

### Presenter notes
- You only ever message Alice; Bob and Cindy stay behind the scenes.
- Expect a brief clarifying/segue line from Alice — that's the "understands before acting" step; it demos well.
- All figures above are computed from the live demo data and match what Bob/Cindy return.
- Everything is one customer (Tom Harper, GV-1012) so the story builds end-to-end.
