How an enquiry flows

Member asks Alice. A member raises any enquiry in plain language — they only ever talk to Alice.
Alice clarifies & classifies. She confirms what's needed and decides: answer directly, or route to a specialist.
Alice routes to Bob or Cindy. Accounts questions go to Bob; marketing questions go to Cindy.
Specialist responds to Alice. Bob or Cindy pulls the data and sends Alice a factual answer.
Alice replies to the member. One friendly, consolidated answer — the hand-offs stay invisible.
