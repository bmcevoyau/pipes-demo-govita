Brand guidelines

Keep Go Vita instantly recognisable. Use the official palette and respect logo clear space.

ColourHexUse
Go Vita Red#EE2528Primary brand colour, logos, headlines, CTAs
Charcoal#282C3FBody text and dark backgrounds
Wellness Green#4C9A5BNatural / health accents, sub-brands
Rewards Gold#F5C518Go Vita Rewards & specials flags
Warm Cream#FFF7EFSoft backgrounds for print
White#FFFFFFReverse logo / clear space

Logo rules: keep clear space equal to the height of the leaf mark · use the reverse logo on dark/photographic backgrounds · never stretch, recolour or add effects · minimum 120px wide on screen (30mm in print). Headlines in heavy sans-serif; tagline line: Your Trusted Wellness Specialist.

How to request an asset

Ask Member Support. Tell our team which asset and format you need (e.g. “the Winter Wellness banner for my website”).
We confirm the details. Support checks sizing and campaign timing, then routes the request to Marketing.
You receive the file. We send the ready-to-use asset (and print specs where relevant) back via Support.
